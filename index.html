Privacy Policy for Toolshi

Last updated: September 25, 2026

Toolshi is a utility application that provides a collection of everyday tools such as calculators, converters, generators, counters, timers, and other utilities.

Toolshi does not require users to create an account or provide personal information such as their name, phone number, or email address to use the application's core utilities.

However, Toolshi uses third-party services, including Google AdMob, which may automatically collect certain information from your device for advertising, analytics, fraud prevention, security, and related purposes.

This information may include information such as:

  • Device and advertising identifiers

  • Diagnostic information

Google AdMob

Toolshi uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. Google and its advertising partners may collect and process information from your device to provide, measure, personalize, and improve advertising, subject to applicable privacy requirements and your consent choices.

You can learn more about how Google uses information from apps that use Google services by visiting Google's privacy and advertising documentation.

Permissions

Some Toolshi utilities may require device permissions in order to provide their functionality. Permissions are requested only when necessary for the relevant feature.

How We Use Information

Information processed through third-party services may be used to:

Toolshi does not sell users' personal information. Information may be processed by third-party service providers used by the application, such as Google AdMob, in accordance with their own privacy policies.

Children's Privacy

Toolshi is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children. If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the application, please contact us so that we can review the matter and take appropriate action.
